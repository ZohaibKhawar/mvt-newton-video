    Mean Value Theorem

    f′(c) =f(b) − f(a)b − a

    Needs f continuous on [a, b] and differentiable on (a, b). Then at least one c in (a, b) works.

    Newton's Method

    xn+1 = xn −f(xn)f′(xn)

    Write the problem as f(x) = 0, pick x0 near the root, repeat. Fails if f′(xn) = 0 or the guess is poor.
